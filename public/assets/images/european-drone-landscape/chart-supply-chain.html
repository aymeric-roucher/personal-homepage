The grip is domain-specific: the aerial stack is China's (which is why a European FPV costs €20,000 against €200 for the Chinese equivalent), while naval and terrestrial subsystems invert to Western strength. Compute is a separate case: only ~5-10% China, but Western-designed chips are etched almost entirely at TSMC and Samsung, a strait away. Sources: RUSI (Nov 2025) and market-structure reports.
