Edge-AI efficiency trend: ~100x per decade (doubling every ~16 months)

GPU modules (NVIDIA Jetson line) Dedicated neural accelerators

Peak inference throughput (TOPS) per watt at each chip's typical power, log scale. The metric follows each era's dominant inference precision (FP16 early, INT8 since ~2018, FP4 on the newest parts), and that precision drop is itself part of the efficiency gain. The fitted trend is ~100x per decade, a doubling every ~16 months: the efficiency that keeps a 7-billion-parameter model off a $448 FPV today stops being the obstacle around the end of the decade (onboard memory and software will lag the raw arithmetic). Sources: vendor datasheets (NVIDIA Jetson, Google Coral, Hailo, Axelera).
