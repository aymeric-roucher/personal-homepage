Two pure tones, 220 Hz and 250 Hz, close enough to grind. With both in both ears they land in the same critical band and fuse into one rough, beating tone. Switch to one tone per ear while it plays: each ear now gets a single clean frequency, nothing to grind against, and the roughness melts into two calm pitches. The tones never changed; only which ear heard what. So the roughness was made in your ear, not in the air.
