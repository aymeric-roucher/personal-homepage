🔊 hold & drag
Top panel: every vertical bar is one partial, placed at its frequency; blue is the fixed note, orange the note you move, and each overtone is dimmer than the one below it. Grab the slider to raise the moving note: its whole ladder of overtones slides rightward, and sound plays while you hold. Watch for the moments where orange bars land exactly on blue ones, their overtones coinciding: it locks into tune. The bottom panel draws the dissonance of every interval you have swept through so far, with the red dot marking where you are: the notches are those in-tune ratios.
