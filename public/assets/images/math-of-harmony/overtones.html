▶ click the wave to hear it
One note at 220 Hz. At zero overtones it is a bare sine wave: a dull electronic whistle. Drag the slider to stack overtones on top (for a string: 2x, 3x, 4x... the fundamental, each quieter): the pitch does not change, but the waveform sharpens and the sound thickens toward something like a plucked string. Then switch the timbre: a metal bar places its overtones at wildly un-integer multiples (2.76x, 5.40x...), which is why a xylophone can never be mistaken for a violin. Click the waveform to start or stop the sound, and change anything while it plays.
