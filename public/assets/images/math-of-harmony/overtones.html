One note at 220 Hz. At zero overtones it is a bare sine wave: a dull electronic whistle. Drag the slider to stack overtones on top (2x, 3x, 4x... the fundamental, each much quieter than the one below it): the pitch does not change, but the waveform sharpens and the sound gains body. Then switch the timbre. Each real instrument has its own recipe of overtone strengths: a flute is almost a pure tone, a clarinet skips the even overtones, a metal bar puts its overtones at un-integer multiples (2.76x, 5.41x...), and a drum membrane crowds them even closer (1.59x, 2.14x, 2.30x...), so unlike a xylophone or a violin it barely has a pitch at all. Click the waveform to start or stop the sound, and change anything while it plays.
