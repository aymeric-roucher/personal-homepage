Each line tracks the running best score on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.1 (9 evaluations, including GDPval-AA v2, Terminal-Bench 2.1, Humanity's Last Exam and GPQA Diamond), across the 15 leading labs; scores before mid-2025 are back-estimates from earlier index versions. Every logo marks the model that set a new record for its camp, labeled with total parameter count; closed labs do not disclose parameter counts. Minor records (under 3% improvement, less than a month after the previous one) are folded into the next step. The axis starts in July 2024, where the frontiers stood at Claude 3 Opus (closed) and Grok-1 (open); the data table below lists every record back to GPT-3.5 in November 2022. Kimi K3's weights are announced but not yet published (due by Jul 27).
