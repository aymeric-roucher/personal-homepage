I've just witnessed the most insane story. Today, the two winners of UTMB - the world’s most mythical ultra-marathon - shared the same secret weapon: a cracked data scientist advisor.

I’m not usually into sports, but this story is epic - and I’m one of the few who know it, because I’m friends with this data scientist!

### Long ago

I’ve known Joseph for years.

He was brilliant at math, but he kept getting fired from every school, because what he cared most about was playing extreme pranks - like turning the schoolyard into an ice skating rink 🧊 I think school wasn't a fun enough challenge - so he preferred to create his own challenges.

### 2022

When starting his career, he surprised everyone : he was excellent at data science and loved sports, so he got comfortable offers at Strava and the like.

But he decided to go with his own challenge : he'd start his own boutique for “performance science”. This meant that he would study all the race’s variables : athlete speed, heart rate, VO2max, wind, declivity. Based on this, he would prepare “pacing plans”, strategic battle plans for dosing the effort along every minute of the race.

To be honest at the beginning… his life seemed just terrible.

Joseph was broke, so he moved to an old house in Chamonix. I went to visit: he had no heating in winter, saw no one, worked 9AM to 3AM, ate pasta every lunch.

❌ He often got rejected by athletes. I mean, who would trust this mathematician out of nowhere, with his glasses?

Once, one of France’s national teams even asked for a paid study, but after he delivered, the old-timers in management heard of it and cancelled payment, because they thought data science was bullshit. (not naming the team, but I hope they’ll settle this someday)

Anyone but him would have given up long ago.

### 2024

By then, Joseph had assembled a small team named Enduraw, and was preparing Ruth Croft 🇳🇿 for UTMB. Experts forecasted her to be ranked ~10.

Joseph prepared an absurd pacing : Ruth would run slowly for half the race.

Ruth trusted him : when the race started, she followed the game plan.
But soon, her confidence started to falter : she was running 40 minutes late on the leaders.

Now, at Grand Col Ferret, Joseph told Ruth to unleash the kraken.

Ruth went ballistic. She caught up, storming past competitors in the last climb of La Flégère - and finished #2!

Of course, Ruth and Joseph were not finished : next on was gold medal.

### 2025

Earlier this year, Joseph was approached by Tom Evans 🇬🇧.

Tom is a world-class british ultra-athlete, but he had a record of abandoning UTMB, in 2023 and 2024.

Tom told Joseph : “Ruth has won UTMB ; I could not even finish. Yet I have lots of things in common with her, even the same coach. Now, the only difference between us is you.” (I swear, it sounds like an action movie)

So, you can imagine I’ve followed closely since UTMB 2025 started yesterday.

- Tom Evans ? ⇒ #1 🥇
- Ruth Croft ? ⇒ #1 🥇

Both ~30 minutes early on the runner-ups.

Joseph Mestrallet is still focusing more on performance than branding ([his website](https://www.joseph-mestrallet.com/) looks like a bad high school project, I'm trying my best to pressure him into upgrading it).

But I'm now positively certain that his work will change endurance sports.
